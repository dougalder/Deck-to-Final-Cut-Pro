Deck to Final Cut Pro

Draft in PowerPoint or Markdown. Get a Final Cut Pro timeline with your text as editable titles, your colors as backgrounds and your images on their own lanes. Motion and transitions are yours to add.

  • Getting your files
  • Keynote: choose File › Export To › PowerPoint to save a .pptx this app can read.
  • Apple Notes: on the latest macOS, choose File › Export as › Markdown to save a note as a .md file.

Private and offline. Everything on this page runs in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server, and it keeps working with your Internet connection turned off. Save this HTML file to keep it for future use.
