# The starter

## What lives in the jar
- Wild yeast
- Lactic acid bacteria
- Flour and water, nothing else
<!-- Open on the jar. Keep this quick, about fifteen seconds, then cut to the close-up. -->

## Feeding it
Twice a day at room temperature, once a week if it lives in the fridge.
^ Mention that cold slows everything down, which is why fridge starters need a day to wake up.

# The dough

## Bulk fermentation
- Four to six hours at 24 °C
- Look for a 50% rise, not a double
